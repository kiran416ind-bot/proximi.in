Our story

Built for founders who ship, not just plan.

Proximi India started with a simple frustration: launching a product in India means duct-taping six tools, paying in dollars, and guessing whether anyone wants what you're building.

We're building the pre-launch marketing team we wished we had — one that creates content, runs a validation-first waitlist, and tracks real engagement, priced in rupees, built for India.

Mission

Help Indian founders validate before they build.

Validate first

Know if people want your product before you spend months building the wrong thing.

India-first

INR billing, IST support, UPI, festivals, Hinglish, WhatsApp Business as a core channel.

Proactive, not passive

AI agents produce the content and insights; you review and approve. Not just another tool.

Values
