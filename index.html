What is Proximi India? ↓

A pre-launch marketing platform for founders and SMBs in India. It creates content, runs a validation-first waitlist, and tracks engagement analytics — priced in rupees, built for India.

Is there a free plan? ↓

Yes — Free tier with 100 signups, 5 interviews, and 10 posts/month. No credit card required.

How does the validation-first waitlist work? ↓

Signup → 3-question survey → auto-booked discovery call → insight dashboard. You learn what people actually want before you build.

Do you bill in INR? ↓

Yes. All plans are billed in Indian rupees via UPI/cards. No forex surprises.

Do you support WhatsApp Business? ↓

Yes — WhatsApp Business is a core channel, with festival-aware content and Hinglish support.

Is my data secure? ↓

Entries are stored via Web3Forms with HTTPS. We never sell your data. You can export or delete anytime.
